Receivers, Amplifiers & Turntables 1977–1983

Every receiver, amplifier and turntable launched between 1977 and 1983 that HiFi-Wiki documents, plus in-depth entries with multilingual sources for the landmark models. Photos load live from Wikimedia Commons where a matching free-licence image exists. Otherwise the card links to a source page with photos.

Photos: taken from the credited source pages (Radiomuseum.org, hifi-wiki, classicreceivers.com, audio-heritage.jp and others) and checked by eye against each model. They are shown for personal reference; the rights stay with their owners, so ask before publishing the page more widely.
